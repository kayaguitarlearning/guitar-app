Reading the tuner

Negative cents means the detected pitch is flat of the nearest note; positive cents means it is sharp. Aim for a stable reading close to 0.

For best results, tune in a quiet room, pluck one string at a time, and let the initial attack settle. The detector uses a pitch-estimation window and smoothing to reduce flicker.
