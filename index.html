The green zone is ±10 cents. A beep sounds when you enter the zone and when you hold it for 0.5 seconds. Use a clean, sustained single note for best results.

How to practise bends

  1. Listen to the target pitch first.
  2. Pluck the starting note clearly.
  3. Bend gradually and watch the meter.
  4. Relax the bend if you go sharp.
  5. Repeat until the pitch feels natural.

Quick reminders

Use standard tuning. Microphone pitch detection can be affected by room noise, vibrato, distortion, and other instruments. The app estimates pitch; it cannot judge your technique or finger placement.

Tuning tips

  1. Reduce background noise.
  2. Pluck one string and let it ring.
  3. If the note is flat, tighten the string slowly.
  4. If the note is sharp, loosen it slowly.
  5. Recheck after each adjustment.

Turn tuning pegs gently. If a string feels unusually tight, stop rather than forcing it.

How to read chord diagrams
  1. Vertical lines represent strings; the leftmost is the low E string.
  2. Horizontal lines represent frets; the thick top line is the nut when shown at the open position.
  3. Numbers in circles indicate frets in the fingering.
  4. “X” means mute the string; “O” means play it open.

Practice ideas

  1. Start at a tempo where you can play cleanly.
  2. Accent the first beat to keep track of the bar.
  3. Try eighth notes or triplets for rhythm practice.
  4. Increase the tempo gradually, not all at once.
